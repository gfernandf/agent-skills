# Sample Input

I need to decide whether we should launch a new SaaS product for legal teams in Spain within the next 12 months.

Context:
- Available budget: €600,000
- Available team: 5 people
- Options to evaluate:

1. Build a full product
2. Launch a pilot/MVP
3. Postpone

- We have experience with B2B SaaS
- We lack experience in legaltech
- There is established competition
- Target market: Spain
- Timeframe: next 12 months

I want a clear recommendation, an evaluation of the alternatives, risks, uncertainties, and level of confidence.
